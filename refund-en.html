Deep End — Refund Policy

Effective date: 2 August 2026

1. Try before you pay

Deep End can be used free of charge, with a weekly message allowance and no account required. Signed-in users can also start a one-time free trial. We would rather you find out whether the Service suits you before paying anything.

2. 14-day refund on subscriptions

If you are not satisfied, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your first subscription payment. You do not need to give a reason.

Renewal payments after the first are refundable within 14 days of the renewal date if you have not used the Service during that period. If you have used it, we may offer a partial refund at our discretion.

3. Message top-ups

One-time top-ups are refundable within 14 days of purchase provided the purchased allowance is still unused. Once the allowance has been partly used, that purchase can no longer be refunded, because what was bought has been delivered.

4. Cancelling instead of refunding

You can cancel a subscription at any time from Settings in the app, which opens the billing portal. Cancelling stops future renewals. Access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you are not charged again. Cancelling on its own does not produce a refund for the current period.

5. If something is broken

If the Service does not work as described — for example replies stop being generated, or paid features do not unlock after payment — please contact us first. We will try to put it right, and if we cannot, we will refund the affected period regardless of the 14-day window.

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com, which is the Merchant of Record for all our orders and handles returns. You can:

  • Open the billing portal from Settings in the app and request it there, or
  • Email us at intellee0707@gmail.com with the email address you used at checkout, or
  • Contact Paddle directly at paddle.net

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. The time it takes to appear depends on your bank or card issuer, and is typically a few working days.

7. Your statutory rights

This policy is in addition to the rights you have under the law of the country where you live, and does not replace them. Consumers in Korea, the EU, the UK and elsewhere may have a statutory right of withdrawal for digital services; where that right is more generous than this policy, the law applies.
